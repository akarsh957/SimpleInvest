# SimpleInvest Presentation Video

**Target length:** about 2 minutes 30 seconds  
**Format:** screen recording with voice-over  
**Demo route:** Dashboard → Markets → AI Screener

## Before recording

- Sign in with a demo account and confirm the dashboard loads.
- Check that market data and the API are available. Quotes may be delayed; only trade when a live quote is shown.
- Use a demo portfolio. Paper trades are simulated, but they change that account's portfolio.
- If the screener shows its sample-data notice, keep that notice visible and describe the results as samples rather than live recommendations.

## Timed script and screen directions

### 0:00–0:20 — Introduction
**On screen:** Open SimpleInvest and show the dashboard.

**Narration:**  
“Meet SimpleInvest: a financial intelligence and paper-trading platform designed to make portfolio research easier to understand. It brings portfolio tracking, market context, AI-assisted research, and simulated trading together in one workspace.”

### 0:20–0:55 — Portfolio dashboard
**On screen:** Point to the portfolio value, invested amount, return, and available cash. Scroll to the performance chart and holdings.

**Narration:**  
“The dashboard starts with the portfolio essentials: current value, invested amount, return, and cash available for paper trades. Below, the performance view provides an illustrative trend with an S&P 500 comparison. The holdings table brings positions and trade actions into one place, while the research note and sector exposure help explain what the portfolio contains.”

### 0:55–1:20 — Scenario analysis and paper trading
**On screen:** Select a different market scenario. Show a holding’s Buy or Sell action and open the order ticket; do not submit an order unless appropriate for the demo account.

**Narration:**  
“Scenario analysis lets us compare how the portfolio could look under different market conditions, such as a base case or a rate shock. For practice, a holding can open a paper-trade order ticket. This is simulated investing—not a real order—and helps demonstrate the workflow without placing a trade in a brokerage account.”

### 1:20–1:55 — Markets and forecast
**On screen:** Open **Markets**, switch between Global, US Markets, or NSE India, then open a stock chart or forecast.

**Narration:**  
“In Markets, we can browse companies across global, US, and Indian listings, compare price changes and trends, and open a company chart. The forecast view presents a model projection, bull and bear boundaries, and confidence context. These are estimates for exploration, not guarantees of future performance.”

### 1:55–2:20 — AI Screener
**On screen:** Open **AI Screener**, briefly show the sector or risk filters, then select a company to display its explanation.

**Narration:**  
“The AI Screener combines research criteria with AI-assisted explanations. We can narrow the results by sector or risk preference, then select a company to inspect the highlighted metrics and growth context. It is a research aid, not personalized financial advice; verify information independently.”

### 2:20–2:35 — Close
**On screen:** Return to the dashboard and hold on the SimpleInvest name.

**Narration:**  
“That’s SimpleInvest: portfolio visibility, market research, and risk-free paper-trading practice in one place. Thanks for watching.”
